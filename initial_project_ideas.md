# Project Ideas

Industries that interest me: social networking, healthcare, environment, fashion  

1. Friends: Uses events api to find events for two users to attend. Become a user with certain interests etc. Match with someone based on interests. Have 1-on-1 conversations with matches. Instead of dating, focuses on friendships. Finds events for matches to attend. Recommends events. Recommends users.
 - OR Uses personality api. Matches users with similar personalities.
2. Skincare: Scrape data from website cosdna.com, cosmetic ingredients website with data on each cosmetic ingredient. I have super sensitive skin and acne-prone skin, and whenever I want to try a new product, I would have to look it up on cosdna.com. However, it gets annoying to do this for every product I'd like to try out. Also, there are companies like Curology that create formula specific to your skin type and skin issues. However, it is costly to maintain a subscription. With my web app, I want users to submit their skin issues and skin type. Then, they can get recommended skin products, skin routines, etc. specific to their skin. Whenever they search for a skincare product, they can immediately know if a product will be okay for their skin instead of having to comb through the data themselves. They can save skincare products they would like to try. They can write reviews. Reviewers and reviews are recommended to user if skin matches by %.
3. Mental Health: Mental health is important to me. Mental healthcare is more expensive for people. I would like my app to help with that. With technology and social media, people like me can feel a life imbalance. Users will make an account and fill out survey. Users will be able to track their mental health by logging in their feelings on a daily basis. They can log their goals and issues they would like to tackle such as: more introspection, more journaling, limiting screen time, etc. They can track certain habits that are harmful or beneficial for their mental health. They can get recommended activities like meditation, journaling, etc. based on their feelings. Users can get recommended articles based on survey and mood changes. Secret chats for users to post anonymously on anything related to mental health.
